PROJECT: Modern Bookstore & Stationery E-commerce Website Prototype

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a beautiful, modern, responsive e-commerce website prototype for a
bookstore and stationery brand.

The store sells books, notebooks, journals, planners, pens, pencils, art
supplies, stickers, and other stationery products.

The website should feel like a creative, cozy, aesthetic bookstore
combined with a curated stationery shop. It should be visually appealing
to young adults, students, readers, and people who love journaling and
cute stationery.

The goal is to create a polished website prototype that looks like a
real modern online bookstore—not a generic AI-generated shop.

The website must have a strong visual identity based on the provided
pink and purple color palette, with clean layouts, attractive product
presentation, and a smooth demo shopping experience.

This is an original design inspired by the visual mood of the provided
Instagram references. Do not clone Instagram or copy another bookstore
website.

IMPORTANT: THIS IS ONLY A PROTOTYPE. Prioritize visual quality, UI/UX,
mock data, and simulated interactions. Do not build unnecessary
production infrastructure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. TARGET USERS

Primary users:

-   Readers who want to discover and buy books.
-   Students looking for notebooks, pens, and school supplies.
-   People interested in journaling, planners, and creative stationery.
-   Customers searching for gifts.
-   Young adults who appreciate aesthetic, colorful, modern products.

The interface should be easy to use for both casual visitors and
customers who know exactly what they want to buy.

3. BRAND / VISUAL IDENTITY

Overall visual direction

Create a soft, playful, editorial bookstore aesthetic.

The visual style should combine:

-   Modern independent bookstore.
-   Cozy reading culture.
-   Aesthetic stationery shop.
-   Soft pastel colors.
-   Editorial typography.
-   Clean e-commerce layouts.
-   Subtle playful details.
-   High-quality product photography.

The website should feel warm, creative, premium, and approachable.

Avoid making it look like a childish toy store or an overly corporate
e-commerce platform.

Color palette

Use this palette as the foundation of the entire website.

The colors are taken from the provided palette reference and may be
adjusted slightly for accessibility and contrast.

Primary colors:

-   Soft Pink: #F8B2B2
-   Dusty Mauve / Rose Purple: #B274A2
-   Muted Purple: #8B659C
-   Deep Indigo: #48458F

Additional supporting colors:

-   Warm Off-White: #FFF9F7
-   Soft Cream: #FFF1EC
-   Dark Plum Text: #30263B
-   Soft Lavender: #E8D7F0
-   Light Pink: #FCE0E0

Color usage:

-   Use soft pink for selected highlights, promotional sections, and
    gentle accents.
-   Use mauve and muted purple for secondary backgrounds and category
    areas.
-   Use deep indigo for primary buttons, important text, navigation
    accents, and strong visual elements.
-   Use warm off-white and cream as the main background colors to keep
    the website comfortable and readable.
-   Use dark plum for body text instead of pure black where appropriate.

Do not use every color everywhere. Maintain a balanced, elegant color
hierarchy.

Important: Text must have sufficient contrast against its background. Do
not place dark purple text on dark purple backgrounds or low-contrast
text on pink backgrounds.

Typography

Use a modern, editorial typography system.

Suggested font pairing:

-   Headings: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or another elegant
    serif font.
-   Body text and interface: Inter, DM Sans, or another clean sans-serif
    font.

Typography should feel like a contemporary independent bookstore.

Use:

-   Large editorial headings.
-   Clear readable product titles.
-   Comfortable body text.
-   Strong visual hierarchy.
-   Generous line-height.
-   Proper spacing between headings and content.

Do not use too many different fonts.

Shapes and layout

-   Soft rounded corners on cards and buttons.
-   Clean rectangular product grids.
-   Spacious layouts.
-   Subtle borders.
-   Occasional organic decorative shapes.
-   Elegant rounded promotional banners.
-   Consistent spacing system.
-   Avoid excessive shadows.
-   Avoid excessive gradients.
-   Avoid clutter.

The website should feel carefully designed, not like a template with
random components.

17. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop

-   Spacious editorial layouts.
-   Multi-column product grids.
-   Full navigation.
-   Sidebar filters.
-   Large hero section.
-   Balanced whitespace.

Tablet

-   Adapt grid columns.
-   Reduce spacing where necessary.
-   Keep navigation usable.
-   Maintain visual hierarchy.
-   Ensure cards remain readable.

Mobile

-   Mobile-first responsive behavior.
-   Hamburger menu.
-   Two-column product grid where appropriate.
-   Full-width buttons when useful.
-   Filter drawer instead of sidebar.
-   Readable typography.
-   Proper touch target sizes.
-   No clipped text.
-   No horizontal page scrolling.
-   Product images remain attractive.
-   Cart and wishlist remain easy to access.

Test the design at approximately:

-   1440px desktop.
-   1024px tablet.
-   768px tablet.
-   390px mobile.
-   320px mobile.

Do not simply shrink the desktop design. Reorganize sections
appropriately for mobile.
